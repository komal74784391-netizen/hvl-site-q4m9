Urban air-quality forecasting is challenging because monitoring stations are sparse and pollutant transport is governed by long-range spatial dependencies. We introduce Lapis, a sparse spatiotemporal graph neural network that models city-scale sensor graphs with learned adjacency and multi-horizon supervision. Lapis reduces forecast error by 18 percent over strong baselines across three metropolitan datasets.
