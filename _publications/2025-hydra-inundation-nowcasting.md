---
title: "Real-Time Inundation Nowcasting with Differentiable Hydrology"
authors: "Miller, R.; Chen, L.; Abara, N."
venue: "NeurIPS 2025"
year: 2025
status: "accepted"
---

We couple a differentiable shallow-water solver with a recurrent assimilation network to produce minute-scale inundation nowcasts from sparse gauge observations. The hybrid model improves flood-extent skill scores while remaining stable under abrupt boundary changes.
