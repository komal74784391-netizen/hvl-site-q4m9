---
title: "Few-Shot Sediment Facies Classification from Core Photographs"
authors: "Miller, R.; Duarte, M.; Sato, K."
venue: "Computers & Geosciences"
year: 2025
status: "published"
---

Sediment facies classification from core photographs is a routine but time-consuming task in reservoir characterisation. We present a few-shot learning pipeline that combines self-supervised feature pretraining with prototype-based classification. The method reaches expert-level agreement using fewer than twenty labelled images per facies.
