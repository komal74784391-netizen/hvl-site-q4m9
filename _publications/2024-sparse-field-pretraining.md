---
title: "Contrastive Pretraining for Sparse Field Observations"
authors: "Miller, R.; Park, S."
venue: "Journal of Machine Learning Research (under review)"
year: 2024
status: "under review"
---

Sparse field observations from environmental sensor networks are difficult to model with standard supervised objectives. We study contrastive pretraining strategies that exploit spatial and temporal neighbourhoods as positive pairs.
