---
title: "Neural ODEs for Aerosol Transport Forecasting"
authors: "Miller, R.; Osei, B.; Novak, J."
venue: "Environmental Modelling & Software"
year: 2025
status: "published"
---

We formulate regional aerosol transport as a continuous-time latent ODE and learn the drift function from satellite and ground-station observations. The model produces skillful multi-day forecasts of particulate matter concentrations at low computational cost.
