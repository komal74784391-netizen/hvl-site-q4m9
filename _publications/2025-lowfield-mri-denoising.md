---
title: "Self-Supervised Denoising for Low-Field MRI"
authors: "Miller, R.; Haddad, Y.; Ivanova, P."
venue: "MICCAI 2025"
year: 2025
status: "accepted"
---

Low-field MRI scanners trade signal-to-noise ratio for accessibility. We propose a self-supervised denoising objective that exploits the statistics of repeated acquisitions and improves downstream segmentation without clean reference volumes.
